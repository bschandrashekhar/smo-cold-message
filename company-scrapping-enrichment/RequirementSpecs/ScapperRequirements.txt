Application type: Streamlit App
Repo: Github
Lead Enrichment to be done using: Apollo.io

Event Prospecting Strategy:

- This tool is a web based application that targets a conference website to extract the list of exhibitors. The input excel sheet will comprise of:

Input: data.xlsx (this will be uploaded to the application front end)
| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Exhibhition | Full name of the exhibhition 
| Exhibitor Link | Link to the list of exhibitors 

Output: 
Step 1:
For each 'Exhibitor Link' from the input excel sheet, I need you to take out the list of companies that are exhibiting and prepare an excel sheet With the following information :

| Company Name | Name of the Exhibiting company.
| Website | Exhibiting Company website URL.
| Location of Company | Geographic location of the Company (city).
| Country | Country of the Company.
| Industry Vertical | Deduce and fill the industry vertical that the company belongs to like Financial Services, Automotive, Banking, Healthcare etc.
| Sub-industry | Sub-vertical or sub-industry within the major vertical if applicable.
| Revenue Range | The latest revenue of the company.

Use #INPUT-STEP1 to as the shortlist criteria.

	#INPUT-STEP1:
	Conditions for the companies to be considered in the above list:
	- Not IT service providers
	- Revenue under $2 billion
	- Preferably financial services (not mandatory)
	- Using technologies in our expertise area which comprises of these: Salesforce, Dell Boomi, Snowflake, .NET, Tableau, MS Fabric.
	- Avoid casting too wide a net on data engineering tools while sticking to the tools within our capabilities.

Step 2:
Within each company, I need to collect the prospects that we can reach out to. The rules for targeting would be role based. Target Contact Shortlisting Strategy (ICP varies by company size):
- Large companies: Director of Engineering, Director of Data Engineering, Head of Applications
- Small/mid-size companies: C-level executives (CEO, CTO, etc.)
- Exclude CFOs from targeting
- Collect contact details and email addresses for identified prospects from lead enrichment services.

Use #INPUT-STEP2 to enhance the Excel template structure with additional data points, the listing has to be ordered based on company:

	#INPUT-STEP2:
	Data Collection Requirements from lead enrichment services.
	- Personal: First name, last name, job title, department
	- Contact: Phone, mobile, email, LinkedIn profile link
	- Location: City, state, country
	- Company: Name, industry, , 
	- Technology: Tools/platforms they use
	- Lead source tracking
